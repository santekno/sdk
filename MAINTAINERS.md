## Package Owners

Security-sensitive packages require review from the Lead Maintainer plus one
additional reviewer. See [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) for the full list.

| Package(s) | Required reviewers |
|------------|-------------------|
| `cryptox/`, `hashx/`, `apikey/`, `jwtx/` | Lead Maintainer + 1 |
| All others | Lead Maintainer |

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who have made significant, sustained contributions over at least
three months may be nominated for maintainer status by the Lead Maintainer.
Maintainers are expected to:

- Review PRs within the 72-hour SLA
- Participate in security triage
- Follow the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct
